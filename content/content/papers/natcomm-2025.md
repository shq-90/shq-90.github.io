# Aerosol Iodide Accelerates Reactive Nitrogen Cycling in the Marine Atmosphere

**Shen H#**, Li Q#, Xu F#, Xue L\*, Hu Y, Saiz-Lopez\*, Wang W, Wang T\*

_Nature Communications_, 2025, 16(1): 8148

## Abstract

We demonstrate that aerosol iodide significantly accelerates reactive nitrogen cycling in the marine atmosphere...

## Key Findings

1. Aerosol iodide converts NO₂ to HONO through heterogeneous reactions
2. This process is a major daytime HONO source in coastal regions
3. The mechanism has significant implications for atmospheric oxidative capacity
